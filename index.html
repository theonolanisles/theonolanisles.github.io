Mechanical design · Motion synthesis · CAD

Concealed hatch hinge and linkage synthesis

A commissioned access-hatch hinge needed a carefully controlled opening path. I could not find a practical tool that would search for linkage geometry directly from the required motion and preferred pivot regions, so I built one - and then developed free browser calculators for others to experiment with.
