The problem: lift and swing a hatch clear

The green panel covers an opening in the dark fixed frame. We want it to rise and turn upwards, rather than merely swing about a conventional butt-hinge pin. Select Open or Closed to inspect the two positions.

Closed body: X = 150 to 450 mm, Y = 254 to 275 mm. Opening edges: X = 140 and 460 mm.

1 · Desired motion

The open pose is calculated by rotating closed coordinates about world origin (0,0), then applying the X and Y translations. Negative rotation turns the hatch anticlockwise on screen.

2 · Preferred pivot positions

Gold markers are draggable in the mechanism viewer. A and D are fixed to the frame; P and Q belong to the moving body. In six-bar mode B, C and E are additional joints. Radius defines how far a solution may depart from the preferred centre.
