Design an opening hatch

First define the door, then position its preferred pivots, prescribe the opening motion, and synthesize a mechanism. Both the illustration and the mechanism viewer use X right and Y up.

1 · Door dimensions

Set the door width and thickness in side elevation. The red door origin O = (0,0) is the right-hand datum, and remains attached to the moving door.

Closed door: X = −width to 0; Y = −thickness to 0. Positive Y points up. Consequently O appears at the upper-right edge of this rectangle; locating it at the lower-right would require thickness to extend in +Y.

The dark frame is illustrative and is not used as collision geometry.

2 · Preferred pivot locations

A and D attach to the fixed frame. P and Q attach to the moving door (or rigid brackets on it). All coordinates refer to the closed position.

Drag the gold targets directly in the drawing or enter exact coordinates. Their default locations are in the +X, −Y quadrant; the test envelope around each is a 50 mm radius.

These are preferred locations. Actual pivots may move within their search circles to achieve the prescribed motion.

3 · Opening movement

Enter the positive magnitude of rotation and choose its direction. The translations are the change of the door origin O from its closed position, not the door centre or a pivot.

In this example the hatch rotates 87.4° clockwise, and O moves 57.6 mm right and 67.8 mm up. The dashed brown outline shows the requested open pose.

4 · Synthesize pivot locations and lengths

Starting at the preferred centres, the search explores outward within the allowed pivot regions. A and D are projected onto the exact geometric constraints; candidates are simulated across their stroke.

Calculate the example linkage.

Kinematic candidates are not validated designs. Check physical thickness, interference, bearings, fasteners, load paths and all intermediate positions in CAD before manufacture.

What the pivots mean

O is the moving-door origin; A and D are fixed, and P and Q move with the door. AP and DQ are rotating links. PQ is the rigid part of the moving door between its pivots.
