A hatch needs to lift and swing clear

Imagine a flat hatch over an opening. A conventional butt hinge might leave the open hatch blocking the opening. We want the hatch to rise and translate as it rotates. The green panel is the hatch and the dark frame is fixed.

This illustration is conceptual; link thickness and fabrication details are not modelled.

1 · Choose motion

All points use millimetres. X increases to the right. Y increases downwards, so negative Y means up. The open pose is a rotation about (0,0), then X/Y translation.

2 · Place preferred pivot targets

Try dragging a gold pivot target in the drawing. Its numeric position updates here. Each dashed circle is the allowed search area, not a physical hole.

A, D: fixed pivots on the frame. P, Q: pivots fixed to the hatch. B, C, E: extra six-bar joints. Radius 0 fixes a target exactly. Candidate pivot locations may differ from the targets.

3 · Find candidate geometry

Hatch example loaded. Search to determine the actual pivot coordinates and link lengths.

Engineering note: These are planar, sampled kinematic candidates, not proven hinge designs. A two-pose requirement admits many mechanisms. Check full-stroke collisions, thickness, loads and bearing clearances in CAD before manufacture. Six-bar searches may find no candidate even when a mechanism is possible.
