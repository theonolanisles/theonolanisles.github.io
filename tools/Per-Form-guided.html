Take it with you

Save the editable design as JSON, or export the closed swept mesh to STL and OBJ. Exports use millimetres. Open paths receive solid end caps. Self-intersections are not repaired; exports are sculptural meshes, not fabrication-certified geometry.

Working with Per-Form

Draw: Press and drag to lay down a freehand stroke, or click for individual corners. Each stroke continues from the existing end.

Move: Drag a highlighted point. Click a segment for length, axis, plane, angle, section transition, twist, grounding and delete-tail. Right-click / Control-click a segment for a quick menu. Double-click to insert a point.

Space: Hold while dragging to orbit. Use the wheel to zoom.

Matching radii: Each corner uses the common radius unless you override it. A bend is shortened as needed to avoid overlapping its neighbours.

Start fresh: Use New form. The Trefoil study button restores a simple symmetrical trefoil knot.
